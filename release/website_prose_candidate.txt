We separate them by what occupies the engineering decision path , with executable membership criteria: E1 — the model fills parameters in a fixed pipeline (replace it with a lookup table and nothing structural changes); E2 — the model plans and executes, but validation criteria, revision logic, and task order are fixed in advance; E3 — the model also diagnoses and revises methods or hypotheses when evidence contradicts them. 智能体三维系统按名字容易比较、按功能难以比较。我们按 谁占据工程决策路径 加以区分，并给出可执行的判据： E1 ——模型在固定流水线中填参数（换成查表不会改变流水线结构）； E2 ——模型规划并执行，但验证标准、修订逻辑和任务顺序预先固定； E3 ——当证据否定其方法或假设时，模型还会诊断并修订它们。 Under this taxonomy the preserved record sits at E3 : B01→B02 revises the evaluation protocol itself, B15 revises the reusable-asset boundary, B23 revises the representation class, and B25/B26 revises task selection — each documented by preserved process files. The behavioral specifics matter more than the summary: it revises hypotheses at the protocol, abstraction, representation, and task-selection layers when evidence contradicts them (F1–F4); it converts sparse human spatial signals into verified multi-step corrections (F5); and its task outputs compose into one editable, semantically extensible station model (F6). 在确定性证据/验证 harness 内，一个通用推理模型能够在真实工业场站上跨 12 个异构任务族充当持续三维工程智能体。比总述更重要的是行为细节：它会在证据否定假设时修订协议、抽象、表示与任务选择层的假设（F1–F4）；能将稀疏人工空间信号转化为经过验证的多步纠错（F5）；其任务输出能组合成一个可编辑、可语义扩展的整站模型（F6）。 Not supported 不支持的结论 Full autonomy — human steering is material and documented (B08, B36, D38). Survey-grade accuracy — all residuals are internal agreement with the correlated photogrammetric reference; no independent survey exists. Model superiority — no matched reruns with another model, a specialized 3D pipeline, or a human CAD baseline were performed. Cross-site generality — one substation, one data-acquisition protocol. The analogy to embodied-agent evaluations is an analogy of reporting structure, not of statistics: these are not repeated randomized episodes, and the validators are heterogeneous by design. 完全自主 ——人工引导是实质性且有记录的（B08、B36、D38）。 测绘级精度 ——所有残差均为与相关摄影测量参考的内部一致性，不存在独立测绘。 模型优越性 ——未与其他模型、专用三维流水线或人工 CAD 基线做对照重跑。 跨站泛化 ——单变电站、单数据采集协议。与具身智能体评估的类比是报告结构上的类比，不是统计上的类比：这些不是重复随机试验，验证器也是按任务异构设计的。 What would change the picture 什么证据会改变当前图景 Matched reruns under the same harness with a second model; an independent LiDAR/total-station reference on a subset of equipment; a second site; per-batch cost ledgers (tokens, wall-clock, human minutes), which the historical record does not contain and which future controlled studies should freeze along with model/version provenance. 同一 harness 下用第二个模型做对照重跑；对部分设备做独立 LiDAR/全站仪参考测量；第二个站点；逐批次成本台账（token、墙钟时间、人工分钟数）——历史记录不包含这些，未来的受控研究应将其与模型/版本 provenance 一并冻结。 9 · Limitations Limitations 局限性 The current record supports a single-site longitudinal behavioral analysis and leaves several questions unresolved. 当前记录支持单站点纵向行为分析，同时保留若干尚未解决的问题。 Single-site external validity. All task families come from one substation; diversity within one site does not establish transfer. 单站点外部效度。 所有任务族来自同一座变电站；站内多样性不能证明可迁移性。 No independent survey reference. Headline centimeter-scale residuals compare against the same mesh that guided reconstruction. They are not independent survey accuracy. 无独立测绘参考。 头条的厘米级残差对照的是同时参与重建的同一网格，不是独立测绘精度。 Heterogeneous task-native validators. Appropriate for an engineering case study; it prevents reduction to one stationary benchmark success rate. 验证器任务异构。 这适合工程案例研究，但也意味着无法归约为单一平稳的 benchmark 成功率。 Human steering is material. Equivalence authorization, omission review, image markup, and semantic audit all influenced the trajectory. 人工引导是实质性的。 同构授权、遗漏审查、图像标记与语义审计都影响了轨迹。 Owner-confirmed model provenance. Historical logs do not freeze model identifier or reasoning effort. Future studies should log model/version, reasoning setting, tool versions, evidence manifest, token usage, wall-clock, human interventions, and output hashes per run. 模型 provenance 为负责人确认。 历史日志未冻结模型标识与推理强度。未来研究应按运行冻结模型/版本、推理设置、工具版本、证据清单、token 用量、墙钟时间、人工干预与输出哈希。 Behavioral mining is filename-level. Revision counts are lower bounds; failure-mode and adaptation labels are manual annotations of documented episodes, reported with their sources, not derived measurements. 行为挖掘是文件名级的。 修订计数是下界；失败模式与适应分类是对建档事件的人工标注，随来源报告，而非派生测量。 References Selected references 主要参考文献 The entries below correspond to the literature cited in the website text.
