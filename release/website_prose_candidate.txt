AstraBuild 研究同一个通用推理智能体能否在持续工程状态中重建异构变电站设备，并保留前序决策对后续任务的影响。 Industrial inspection and maintenance require more structure than a fused reconstruction surface provides. The target representation must preserve editable components, repeated asset relationships, site-specific connections, and stable identifiers that can later support semantic and inspection annotations. Registered photogrammetric geometry supplies spatial layout and approximate metric support, but it does not identify component boundaries or reusable engineering structure. 工业巡检和维护所需的结构信息超过融合重建表面本身能够提供的内容。目标表示需要保留可编辑部件、重复资产关系、站点特有连接和稳定标识，以便后续接入语义与巡检标注。注册摄影测量几何提供空间布局和近似尺度，但不会自动给出部件边界或可复用工程结构。 The available evidence is also uneven. The coarse mesh contains holes, fused neighboring objects, and limited component detail. Field images reveal visible form and ordering but are not uniformly calibrated. Inventory and 12MZ records provide expected identities and inspection concepts without guaranteeing that the corresponding geometry has been reconstructed. Human review can identify omissions and implausible structures, but it supplies sparse corrections rather than a complete geometric specification. 现有证据也并不均一。粗模存在孔洞、邻近对象融合和部件细节不足；现场图像能够提供可见形态与排列关系，但并非全部严格标定；设备台账和 12MZ 记录给出预期身份与巡视概念，却不能保证对应几何已经完成重建；人工审查能够指出遗漏和不合理结构，但提供的是稀疏纠错信号，而非完整几何规范。 The study follows a single GPT-6 Astra/Codex workflow across this evidence regime. Each task inherits the existing Blender scene, reusable assets, prior validators, and recorded limitations. The primary research question is: Can one general-purpose reasoning agent operate across heterogeneous component-level reconstruction tasks and compose their outputs into a persistent industrial digital twin? 本研究在上述证据条件下持续使用同一 GPT-6 Astra/Codex 工作流。每个任务继承现有 Blender 场景、可复用资产、前序验证器与已记录限制。本文的主要研究问题是：同一个通用推理智能体能否跨异构部件级重建任务工作，并将这些任务的输出组合为持续演化的工业数字孪生？ 1.1 Contributions1.1 主要贡献 Persistent 3D engineering formulation. The recorded sequence therefore supports a description of AstraBuild as a persistent engineering workflow within this site. The evidence does not establish how the same workflow would transfer to a different substation or to a different reconstruction domain. 后续批次持续使用早期批次产生的几何、部件库、验证器和已知限制。因此，在当前站点范围内，现有记录支持将 AstraBuild 描述为持续工程工作流；但这些证据不能说明该工作流在另一座变电站或其他重建领域中的迁移表现。 8.2 Validation changes representation choices8.2 验证会改变表示选择 Several revisions alter more than numerical parameters. Geometry residuals, history preservation, rigid-instance checks, contact or continuity checks, coverage audits, and review records provide independent execution-time constraints on the generated programs. Their scope remains limited by the available reference data: in particular, photogrammetric residuals measure agreement with a correlated reconstruction rather than absolute survey accuracy. 该工作流不以推理模型作为唯一验收机制。几何残差、历史保持、刚体实例检查、接触或连续性检查、覆盖审计和 review 记录共同对生成程序施加独立的执行期约束。这些检查仍受参考数据范围限制，尤其是摄影测量残差衡量的是与相关重建结果的一致性，而非绝对测绘精度。 9 · Limitations Limitations and threats to validity局限性与有效性威胁 The current record supports a single-site longitudinal analysis and leaves several questions unresolved. 当前记录支持单站点纵向分析，同时保留若干尚未解决的问题。 Site scope. All reconstruction tasks come from one 220 kV substation. Task diversity within this site provides no direct estimate of cross-site generalization. 站点范围。 所有重建任务均来自同一座 220 kV 变电站，因此站内任务多样性不能直接估计跨站泛化能力。 Reference geometry. The photogrammetric mesh guides reconstruction and is also used in the reported reference-surface comparisons. The residual statistics therefore quantify internal agreement with that reconstruction, not independent survey accuracy. 参考几何。 摄影测量粗模既用于指导重建，也用于报告参考表面对照，因此残差统计衡量的是与该重建结果的内部一致性，而不是独立测绘精度。 Human intervention. Human decisions affect the B08 equivalence assumption, B36 omission review, D38 markup, and semantic audit. The present study describes a human-steerable workflow and does not estimate performance under fully autonomous operation. 人工介入。 B08 的同构判断、B36 的遗漏审查、D38 标记以及语义审计均受到人工决策影响。因此本文描述的是人工可引导工作流，并未估计完全自主运行条件下的性能。 Heterogeneous validation. Geometry, connectivity, civil reconstruction, correction, and semantic tasks use different validators. The historical record does not provide a common repeated-trial denominator from which a single success rate can be estimated. 异构验证。 几何、连接、土建、纠错和语义任务使用不同验证器。历史记录不存在可用于估计统一成功率的共同重复试验分母。 Model provenance and comparison.
