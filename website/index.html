Summary摘要

Research question. Can one general-purpose reasoning model operate as a long-horizon 3D engineering agent — solving heterogeneous component-level reconstruction tasks and composing their outputs into a persistent, evolving industrial digital twin? The model under test is GPT-6 Astra; the harness around it is the method, and throughout this report we do not attribute harness behavior to the model or model behavior to the harness.

研究问题。一个通用推理模型能否作为长时程三维工程智能体——完成异构的部件级重建任务，并把任务输出组合成持续演化的工业数字孪生？被测对象是 GPT-6 Astra；围绕它的 harness 是方法。本报告始终区分二者：不把 harness 的行为记在模型头上，也不把模型的行为记在 harness 头上。

Protocol, stated up front. One model, one operating 220 kV substation, 38 longitudinal core batches across 12 task families. The batches are not IID trials: later tasks inherit geometry, reusable assets, validators, and recorded failures from earlier ones. We therefore report behavioral and task-native evidence, not a benchmark success rate. Model provenance is owner-confirmed (see hero note); behavioral statistics are mined from the preserved process record, not remembered.

协议透明化。单一模型、单一运行中 220 kV 变电站、跨 12 个任务族的 38 个纵向核心批次。这些批次不是 IID 试验：后续任务继承前序的几何、可复用资产、验证器和已记录失败。因此本报告给出行为与任务原生证据，而不是 benchmark 成功率。模型 provenance 为负责人确认（见页首注）；行为统计来自对冻结过程记录的挖掘，而非回忆。

The central finding is behavioral: a general-purpose reasoning model may indicate the ability to operate a persistent engineering process — it revises evaluation protocols, abstraction boundaries, representation classes, and task-selection strategies when evidence contradicts them, through traceable, preserved process artifacts. The evidence does not show full autonomy (human steering is material), survey-grade accuracy (no independent reference), or model superiority (no matched reruns).

核心发现是行为层面的：通用推理模型或可表明具备运行持续工程过程的能力——当证据否定其假设时，它会通过可追溯、被冻结的过程产物修订评估协议、抽象边界、表示类别和任务选择策略。现有证据不支持完全自主（人工引导是实质性的）、测绘级精度（无独立参考）或模型优越性（无对照重跑）的结论。

1 · Setup

The model under test, and the harness around it被测模型与其外围 harness

"Can a general-purpose model do industrial 3D reconstruction?" is not a single question, because the model is never the whole system. This evaluation is arranged so that the setup does not answer the question for the model.

“通用模型能否做工业三维重建”不是一个单一问题，因为模型从来不是整个系统。本评估的设计原则是：不让实验装置替模型回答问题。

Provenance and inference boundary.Provenance 与推断边界。 The historical reconstruction used Codex + GPT-6 Astra with Extra High reasoning effort, as owner-confirmed experimental metadata; the old batch files do not freeze this. The batches also do not preserve the model's private chain-of-thought. What they preserve is an observable engineering decision trail — selected evidence, authored or revised programs, deterministic outputs, validation results, and subsequent revisions. All behavioral claims in this report are made over that observable trail.历史重建使用 Codex + GPT-6 Astra（Extra High 推理强度），属项目负责人确认的实验元数据，旧批次文件未冻结该信息。批次同样不保存模型的内部思维链。它们保存的是一条可观测的工程决策轨迹——被选中的证据、编写或修订的程序、确定性输出、验证结果与后续修订。本报告的全部行为结论都建立在这条可观测轨迹之上。

Agentic 3D systems are easy to compare by name and hard to compare by function. We separate them by what occupies the engineering decision path, with executable membership criteria: E1 — the model fills parameters in a fixed pipeline (replace it with a lookup table and nothing structural changes); E2 — the model plans and executes, but validation criteria, revision logic, and task order are fixed in advance; E3 — the model also diagnoses and revises methods or hypotheses when evidence contradicts them.

智能体三维系统按名字容易比较、按功能难以比较。我们按谁占据工程决策路径加以区分，并给出可执行的判据：E1——模型在固定流水线中填参数（换成查表不会改变流水线结构）；E2——模型规划并执行，但验证标准、修订逻辑和任务顺序预先固定；E3——当证据否定其方法或假设时，模型还会诊断并修订它们。

Under this taxonomy the preserved record sits at E3: B01→B02 revises the evaluation protocol itself, B15 revises the reusable-asset boundary, B23 revises the representation class, and B25/B26 revises task selection — each documented by preserved process files. E3 describes who revises the hypotheses, not who watches: two of seven adaptation episodes were initiated by sparse human signals, and one required explicit human authorization.

按此分类，本记录位于 E3：B01→B02 修订了评估协议本身，B15 修订可复用资产边界，B23 修订表示类别，B25/B26 修订任务选择——每一处都有冻结的过程文件佐证。E3 描述的是谁在修订假设，而不是谁在旁观：7 次适应事件中有 2 次由稀疏人工信号触发，1 次需要明确人工授权。

3.2 A capability ladder3.2 能力阶梯

Rung 1 — Semantic grounding is reliable第一级——语义接地可靠

Inventory names, 12MZ groups, and inspection concepts were attached to geometry with explicit accounting: 151 devices in the part map, 76 of 106 official names attached, 30 deliberately left unmatched. A later semantic audit caught naïve string/box attachments and revised them.

台账名称、12MZ 分组与巡视概念以显式记账方式附着到几何：部件图含 151 台设备，106 个官方名称中 76 个完成附着，30 个有意留作未匹配。后续语义审计发现了朴素的字符串/包围盒附着并予以修订。

Rung 2 — Metric envelope fitting is reliable when a measurement operator runs第二级——有测量算子运行时，度量包络拟合可靠

Where the agent compiled a local registered reference and fitted parametric envelopes — walls (east-wall median 7.87 m→0.015 m), capacitor lanes (18 medians 0.6–2.4 cm), building wall domains (RMS 1.8–3.2 cm), B32 cabins — geometry lands at centimeter-level internal agreement. The fits are deterministic; the agent's contribution is deciding what to fit, on which domain, with which split.

凡智能体编译了局部注册参考并拟合参数化包络之处——围墙（东墙中位数 7.87 m→0.015 m）、电容器阵列（18 个中位数 0.6–2.4 cm）、建筑墙面域（RMS 1.8–3.2 cm）、B32 设备舱——几何均达到厘米级内部一致性。拟合是确定性计算；智能体的贡献在于决定拟合什么、在哪个域上、按什么划分。

Rung 3 — Fine visible detail needs review rounds第三级——细节层可见结构需要审查轮次

Door count/side/position in B32 required R1→R2→R3 corrections driven by same-camera review; the B01 arrester failure came from accessory geometry contaminating the comparison domain; T2's front retains a P95 0.62 m residual tail that summary statistics would hide. Detail errors are semantic-perceptual (what is this feature?), not numeric.

B32 门的数量/朝向/位置需要同机位审查驱动的 R1→R2→R3 修订；B01 避雷器失败源于附件几何污染比较域；T2 正面保留着 P95 0.62 m 的残差尾部，汇总统计会将其掩盖。细节错误是语义—感知性的（这个结构是什么？），而非数值性的。

Rung 4 — Connectivity and coverage need dedicated operators第四级——连接性与覆盖度需要专用算子

Bus/conductor systems required route diagnostics and endpoint-continuity checks (B20; continuity ≈1e-6 m); unknown omissions required the coverage audit as an active sensor (B25/B26). Neither emerges from per-device fitting.

母线/导线系统需要路由诊断与端点连续性检查（B20；连续性 ≈1e-6 m）；未知遗漏需要把覆盖审计当作主动传感器（B25/B26）。二者都不会从逐设备拟合中自发产生。

7 · System composition

System-level composition and state discipline系统级组合与状态纪律

Composition is a separate capability (Finding 6): shared masters must stay shared only where equivalence holds, earlier transforms and protected artifacts must survive later batches, and presentation must stay decoupled from geometry.

组合是独立的能力考验（发现 F6）：共享 master 仅在同构成立处保持共享，早期变换与受保护产物必须在后续批次中存活，展示必须与几何解耦。

The record shows this discipline holding at scale: 2,859+ protected-file hashes by the B35-era chain; B32's validator checks 34,191 inherited objects and 2,953 protected files; failed variants are preserved rather than cleaned up. At the D38.2/D40-equivalent layer the composed project contains 37,153 objects, 755 scenes, 858 collections, 3,916 mesh datablocks, and 29,986,649 vertices — a descriptive object-to-mesh reuse ratio of ≈9.5×. Reconstructed coverage includes 48 arrester bodies, 8+3 GIS bays, two main transformers, six capacitor groups, ≈88.8 m of busbar, 804 insulator discs, ≈10,182 m² of ground surface, and a 946-component main building. These counts demonstrate the breadth of one persistent process; they do not imply that every physical object is independently verified.

记录显示这种纪律在规模下成立：B35 时期链条已有 2,859+ 个受保护文件哈希；B32 的验证器检查 34,191 个继承对象与 2,953 个受保护文件；失败变体被保留而非清理。D38.2/D40 等价层的组合工程包含 37,153 个对象、755 个 scene、858 个 collection、3,916 个 mesh datablock 和 29,986,649 个顶点——描述性对象/网格复用比约 9.5×。重建覆盖范围包括 48 支避雷器本体、8+3 个 GIS 间隔、两台主变、六组电容器、约 88.8 m 母线、804 片绝缘子、约 10,182 m² 地坪与 946 部件的主控楼。这些数量说明单个持续过程的操作广度，并不意味着每个物理对象都经过独立验证。

8 · Discussion

What this evaluation does and does not show本评估支持与不支持的结论

Claims are graded by what the preserved record can carry.

结论按冻结记录能够支撑的强度分级。

Supported支持的结论

A general-purpose reasoning model, inside a deterministic evidence/validation harness, can operate as a persistent 3D engineering agent across 12 heterogeneous task families at one real industrial site. The behavioral specifics matter more than the summary: it revises hypotheses at the protocol, abstraction, representation, and task-selection layers when evidence contradicts them (F1–F4); it converts sparse human spatial signals into verified multi-step corrections (F5); and its task outputs compose into one editable, semantically extensible station model (F6).

在确定性证据/验证 harness 内，一个通用推理模型能够在真实工业场站上跨 12 个异构任务族充当持续三维工程智能体。比总述更重要的是行为细节：它会在证据否定假设时修订协议、抽象、表示与任务选择层的假设（F1–F4）；能将稀疏人工空间信号转化为经过验证的多步纠错（F5）；其任务输出能组合成一个可编辑、可语义扩展的整站模型（F6）。

Not supported不支持的结论

Full autonomy — human steering is material and documented (B08, B36, D38). Survey-grade accuracy — all residuals are internal agreement with the correlated photogrammetric reference; no independent survey exists. Model superiority — no matched reruns with another model, a specialized 3D pipeline, or a human CAD baseline were performed. Cross-site generality — one substation, one data-acquisition protocol. The analogy to embodied-agent evaluations is an analogy of reporting structure, not of statistics: these are not repeated randomized episodes, and the validators are heterogeneous by design.

完全自主——人工引导是实质性且有记录的（B08、B36、D38）。测绘级精度——所有残差均为与相关摄影测量参考的内部一致性，不存在独立测绘。模型优越性——未与其他模型、专用三维流水线或人工 CAD 基线做对照重跑。跨站泛化——单变电站、单数据采集协议。与具身智能体评估的类比是报告结构上的类比，不是统计上的类比：这些不是重复随机试验，验证器也是按任务异构设计的。

What would change the picture什么证据会改变当前图景

Matched reruns under the same harness with a second model; an independent LiDAR/total-station reference on a subset of equipment; a second site; per-batch cost ledgers (tokens, wall-clock, human minutes), which the historical record does not contain and which future controlled studies should freeze along with model/version provenance.

同一 harness 下用第二个模型做对照重跑；对部分设备做独立 LiDAR/全站仪参考测量；第二个站点；逐批次成本台账（token、墙钟时间、人工分钟数）——历史记录不包含这些，未来的受控研究应将其与模型/版本 provenance 一并冻结。

9 · Limitations

Limitations局限性

The current record supports a single-site longitudinal behavioral analysis and leaves several questions unresolved.

当前记录支持单站点纵向行为分析，同时保留若干尚未解决的问题。

Single-site external validity. All task families come from one substation; diversity within one site does not establish transfer.

单站点外部效度。所有任务族来自同一座变电站；站内多样性不能证明可迁移性。

No independent survey reference. Headline centimeter-scale residuals compare against the same mesh that guided reconstruction. They are not independent survey accuracy.

无独立测绘参考。头条的厘米级残差对照的是同时参与重建的同一网格，不是独立测绘精度。

Heterogeneous task-native validators. Appropriate for an engineering case study; it prevents reduction to one stationary benchmark success rate.

验证器任务异构。这适合工程案例研究，但也意味着无法归约为单一平稳的 benchmark 成功率。

Human steering is material. Equivalence authorization, omission review, image markup, and semantic audit all influenced the trajectory.

人工引导是实质性的。同构授权、遗漏审查、图像标记与语义审计都影响了轨迹。

Owner-confirmed model provenance. Historical logs do not freeze model identifier or reasoning effort. Future studies should log model/version, reasoning setting, tool versions, evidence manifest, token usage, wall-clock, human interventions, and output hashes per run.

模型 provenance 为负责人确认。历史日志未冻结模型标识与推理强度。未来研究应按运行冻结模型/版本、推理设置、工具版本、证据清单、token 用量、墙钟时间、人工干预与输出哈希。

Behavioral mining is filename-level. Revision counts are lower bounds; failure-mode and adaptation labels are manual annotations of documented episodes, reported with their sources, not derived measurements.

行为挖掘是文件名级的。修订计数是下界；失败模式与适应分类是对建档事件的人工标注，随来源报告，而非派生测量。
