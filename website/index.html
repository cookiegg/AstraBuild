Abstract

Abstract摘要

This study examines a GPT-6 Astra/Codex workflow for component-level reconstruction of an operating 220 kV substation. The workflow receives registered photogrammetric geometry, field images, engineering records, and the persistent state produced by earlier tasks. It modifies that state through Blender/Python programs, while task-specific deterministic checks determine whether a revision is retained, revised again, or quarantined. The study is longitudinal: later tasks inherit geometry, reusable assets, validators, and recorded failures from earlier batches.

本文研究 GPT-6 Astra/Codex 工作流在运行中 220 kV 变电站部件级重建任务中的使用。工作流接收注册摄影测量几何、现场图像、工程记录以及前序任务形成的持续工程状态，并通过 Blender/Python 程序修改该状态；任务专用的确定性检查决定某次修订是被保留、继续修订还是隔离。研究采用纵向设计，后续任务继承早期批次的几何、可复用资产、验证器和已记录失败。

The record contains 38 core reconstruction batches covering 12 task families. Thirty-two batches preserve the original interactive review pages used to compare editable models with registered coarse geometry under controlled views. At the D38.2 geometry baseline, the station contains 37,153 objects, 755 scenes, 858 collections, and 3,916 mesh datablocks. Preserved revisions document changes to the fitting/holdout domain, reusable-asset boundaries, geometric representation, task selection from unexplained coverage, and the interpretation of human review markup. D41 subsequently adds 79 accessory meshes from 17 selected inspection-relevant classes to both transformer instances.

现有记录包含 38 个核心重建批次和 12 类任务。其中 32 个批次保留了实验当时生成的交互式 review 页面，用于在受控视角下比较可编辑模型与注册粗模。D38.2 几何基线包含 37,153 个对象、755 个 scene、858 个 collection 和 3,916 个 mesh datablock。保留的修订记录显示，工作流曾修改拟合/留出域、可复用资产边界、几何表示、基于未解释覆盖度的任务选择，以及对人工审查标记的解释。D41 在此基础上向两台主变实例加入 79 个附件网格，覆盖 17 类选定巡检相关部件。

These observations characterize the workflow as a persistent 3D engineering agent within one site. They do not establish survey-grade geometric accuracy, full autonomy, cross-site generalization, or a matched comparison with other models. Reference-surface residuals use the same photogrammetric reconstruction that also guides modeling and therefore are not independent survey accuracy.

这些观察支持将该工作流描述为单站点内的持续三维工程智能体，但不能据此推断测绘级几何精度、完全自主性、跨站泛化能力或相对于其他模型的受控比较优势。参考表面残差使用了同时参与建模的摄影测量重建，因此不构成独立测绘精度。

1 · Introduction

Problem setting and research question问题背景与研究问题

AstraBuild studies whether one general-purpose reasoning agent can reconstruct heterogeneous substation equipment within a persistent engineering state and retain the consequences of earlier decisions.

AstraBuild 研究同一个通用推理智能体能否在持续工程状态中重建异构变电站设备，并保留前序决策对后续任务的影响。

Industrial inspection and maintenance require more structure than a fused reconstruction surface provides. The target representation must preserve editable components, repeated asset relationships, site-specific connections, and stable identifiers that can later support semantic and inspection annotations. Registered photogrammetric geometry supplies spatial layout and approximate metric support, but it does not identify component boundaries or reusable engineering structure.

工业巡检和维护所需的结构信息超过融合重建表面本身能够提供的内容。目标表示需要保留可编辑部件、重复资产关系、站点特有连接和稳定标识，以便后续接入语义与巡检标注。注册摄影测量几何提供空间布局和近似尺度，但不会自动给出部件边界或可复用工程结构。

The available evidence is also uneven. The coarse mesh contains holes, fused neighboring objects, and limited component detail. Field images reveal visible form and ordering but are not uniformly calibrated. Inventory and 12MZ records provide expected identities and inspection concepts without guaranteeing that the corresponding geometry has been reconstructed. Human review can identify omissions and implausible structures, but it supplies sparse corrections rather than a complete geometric specification.

现有证据也并不均一。粗模存在孔洞、邻近对象融合和部件细节不足；现场图像能够提供可见形态与排列关系，但并非全部严格标定；设备台账和 12MZ 记录给出预期身份与巡视概念，却不能保证对应几何已经完成重建；人工审查能够指出遗漏和不合理结构，但提供的是稀疏纠错信号，而非完整几何规范。

The study follows a single GPT-6 Astra/Codex workflow across this evidence regime. Each task inherits the existing Blender scene, reusable assets, prior validators, and recorded limitations. The primary research question is: Can one general-purpose reasoning agent operate across heterogeneous component-level reconstruction tasks and compose their outputs into a persistent industrial digital twin?

本研究在上述证据条件下持续使用同一 GPT-6 Astra/Codex 工作流。每个任务继承现有 Blender 场景、可复用资产、前序验证器与已记录限制。本文的主要研究问题是：同一个通用推理智能体能否跨异构部件级重建任务工作，并将这些任务的输出组合为持续演化的工业数字孪生？

1.1 Contributions1.1 主要贡献

  1. Persistent 3D engineering formulation. The reconstruction process is represented as an inherited engineering state modified through programmatic actions and checked by deterministic validators.
  2. Heterogeneous longitudinal task suite. One workflow is documented across 12 equipment and infrastructure task families and 38 core batches in the same operating substation.
  3. Auditable intermediate evidence. Measurement profiles, diagnostics, original review pages, validation records, failed revisions, and continuation notes preserve the evidence used to accept or revise geometry.
  4. Revision analysis. Preserved cases document changes to evaluation domains, reusable-asset boundaries, geometric representations, coverage-based task selection, and interpretation of human feedback.
  5. Station-scale integration. The retained task outputs accumulate into a 37,153-object station model and support the later D41 inspection-semantic augmentation.
  1. 持续三维工程建模表述。 将重建过程表示为可继承的工程状态，通过程序化动作修改，并由确定性验证器检查。
  2. 异构纵向任务集。 在同一运行变电站中记录同一个工作流跨 12 类设备/设施任务和 38 个核心批次的执行过程。
  3. 可审计中间证据。 measurement profile、诊断图、原始 review 页面、validation 记录、失败修订和 continuation note 保留几何被接受或修订时所依据的证据。
  4. 修订行为分析。 保留案例记录了评估域、可复用资产边界、几何表示、基于覆盖度的任务选择以及人工反馈解释的变化。
  5. 整站级集成。 被保留的任务输出累计形成 37,153 对象的整站模型，并进一步支持 D41 巡检语义增强。

1.2 Related work and positioning1.2 相关工作与研究定位

Structure-from-motion, NeRF, and 3D Gaussian Splatting recover scene geometry or view-dependent appearance from images [1–3]. In this study, the photogrammetric reconstruction is used as a registered reference and spatial scaffold; the engineering output is a componentized Blender state with reusable assets, site instances, explicit connections, review artifacts, and semantic mappings.

Structure-from-motion、NeRF 和 3D Gaussian Splatting 从图像恢复场景几何或视角相关外观 [1–3]。在本文中，摄影测量重建被用作注册参考和空间骨架；工程输出则是包含可复用资产、站点实例、显式连接、审查产物和语义映射的部件化 Blender 状态。

3D-GPT and SceneCraft use language models to produce procedural 3D scenes and executable Blender programs [4,5]. AstraBuild addresses a reverse-engineering setting in which registered physical evidence constrains geometry and later revisions. GPT-Policy and the GPT-6 Astra embodied-policy report provide the closest experimental analogy: one general-purpose model operates repeatedly in a common environment, receives execution feedback, and is assessed across heterogeneous tasks [6,7]. The present study transfers that agent-centric evaluation perspective to a persistent 3D engineering environment.

3D-GPT 和 SceneCraft 使用语言模型生成程序化三维场景和可执行 Blender 程序 [4,5]。AstraBuild 面向逆向工程场景，注册后的物理证据持续约束几何与后续修订。GPT-Policy 与 GPT-6 Astra embodied-policy 报告提供了最接近的实验类比：同一个通用模型在共同环境中反复执行任务、接收执行反馈，并在异构任务上被观察 [6,7]。本文将这一智能体评估视角转移到持续三维工程环境中。

Inference boundary.推断边界。 Historical batches preserve scripts, selected inputs, diagnostic figures, renders, revisions, validators, and continuation notes. They do not preserve GPT-6 Astra's private chain-of-thought. The analysis is therefore limited to the recorded engineering trace: selected evidence, authored or revised programs, deterministic outputs, validation results, and subsequent revisions.历史批次保存脚本、选定输入、诊断图、渲染、修订、验证器和续作记录，但不保存 GPT-6 Astra 的内部思维链。因此本文仅分析可记录的工程轨迹：被选择的证据、编写或修改的程序、确定性输出、验证结果及后续修订。

For task t, the context contains registered local geometry, field images, engineering or semantic records, spatial priors, and the accumulated batch history. The recorded model outputs are programs and revisions to programs. These programs extract measurements, create or modify Blender objects, instantiate reusable assets, generate connections, render review views, or compute validation inputs.

对于任务 t，上下文包括注册局部几何、现场图像、工程或语义记录、空间先验以及累计批次历史。历史中可记录的模型输出主要是程序及其修订。这些程序用于提取测量量、创建或修改 Blender 对象、实例化可复用资产、生成连接、渲染审查视图或计算验证输入。

Numerical fitting and rendering are executed outside the language model. NumPy/SciPy/Matplotlib generate measurements and diagnostic plots; Blender creates component geometry and fixed-camera renders; independent validation scripts recompute task-specific checks. A batch is promoted to the inherited state only after the corresponding release and validation records are written. Failed or limited revisions remain available to later tasks.

数值拟合与渲染在语言模型之外执行。NumPy/SciPy/Matplotlib 生成测量结果和诊断图，Blender 生成部件几何和固定机位渲染，独立验证脚本重新计算任务专用检查。只有在相应 release 和 validation 记录写入后，批次结果才进入后续任务继承的工程状态；失败或受限修订继续保留供后续任务查阅。

2.3 · Methods

Agent actions, validation, and external memory智能体动作、验证与外部记忆

The workflow separates model-authored engineering actions from deterministic execution and from the records used to carry state across tasks.

该工作流将模型编写的工程动作、确定性执行过程以及跨任务保存状态的记录分开处理。

Recorded actions include extraction of local references, transform fitting, parametric component construction, creation of shared MASTER collections, site instantiation, connector and conductor generation, review rendering, quarantine of suspect objects, and release of checked artifacts. The available action set expands with the scripts already present in the project rather than being fixed in advance.

历史中记录的动作包括局部参考提取、变换拟合、参数化部件构建、共享 MASTER collection 创建、站点实例化、连接件与导线生成、审查渲染、可疑对象隔离以及已检查产物的发布。可用动作集合会随项目中已有脚本扩展，并非预先固定。

Plans, builders, validators, manifests, review renders, failed drafts, and continuation notes form the external memory used by later tasks. These files record both retained geometry and known limitations. The longitudinal analysis therefore follows changes to this persistent record in addition to changes visible in the Blender scene.

plan、builder、validator、manifest、review render、失败草稿和 continuation note 构成后续任务使用的外部记忆。这些文件同时记录被保留的几何和已知限制。因此纵向分析不仅跟踪 Blender 场景的变化，也跟踪这一持续记录本身的变化。

3 · Experimental Setup

Study design and reconstruction tasks研究设计与重建任务

The study follows one operating substation through a longitudinal sequence of dependent reconstruction tasks.

本研究跟踪同一运行变电站中的一系列纵向依赖重建任务。

3.1 Reconstruction task families3.1 重建任务族

The 38 core batches are longitudinally dependent. Each batch starts from the engineering state left by its predecessor, including the Blender scene, reusable component libraries, validators, and recorded limitations. The 12 task families summarize the range of work performed within this common state: local device fitting, repeated assemblies, reusable GIS families, connected conductor systems, civil structures, auxiliary infrastructure, correction tasks, presentation control, and inspection-semantic augmentation.

38 个核心批次具有纵向依赖关系。每个批次从前一批次留下的工程状态开始，包括 Blender 场景、可复用部件库、验证器和已记录限制。12 类任务概括了这一共同状态中的工作范围：局部设备拟合、重复总成、可复用 GIS 设备族、连接导线系统、土建结构、辅助设施、纠错任务、展示层控制和巡检语义增强。

The sequence also records a change in reconstruction scope. B01–B19 concentrate on individual devices and reusable equipment families. B20–B30 add explicit bus and conductor connections and use coverage analysis to identify unresolved geometry. B31–D38 close civil and auxiliary structures and incorporate reviewer-identified omissions. D40 preserves geometry while changing presentation materials, and D41 adds transformer inspection semantics.

任务序列还记录了重建范围的变化。B01–B19 主要处理单体设备和可复用设备族；B20–B30 加入显式母线与导线连接，并使用覆盖分析定位未解决几何；B31–D38 完成土建与辅助设施，并处理审查者指出的遗漏；D40 在保持几何不变的情况下修改展示材质；D41 增加主变巡检语义。

3.2 · Experimental Setup

Review protocol and task-specific validation审查协议与任务专用验证

Qualitative review uses a common matched-camera convention; quantitative checks are defined for the geometry and failure mode of each task family.

定性审查采用统一的同机位约定；定量检查则根据不同任务族的几何性质与失败模式分别定义。

Under each fixed review camera, Clean contains the modeled asset, Overlay combines the model with the registered reference, and Reference contains the source geometry alone. The numerical checks follow the object being reconstructed. Arrester and equipment-surface batches use held-out or fixed reference domains. Conductor batches use endpoint continuity, support, and contact checks. 8 · Discussion

Interpretation of the longitudinal record纵向记录的解释

The discussion separates observations supported by the recorded batches from broader claims that require additional experiments.

本节区分历史批次直接支持的观察结果与仍需额外实验支持的更广泛结论。

8.1 Persistent state extends the task horizon8.1 持续状态延长了任务时间尺度

Later batches routinely consume geometry, component libraries, validators, and limitations created by earlier batches. The recorded sequence therefore supports a description of AstraBuild as a persistent engineering workflow within this site. The evidence does not establish how the same workflow would transfer to a different substation or to a different reconstruction domain.

后续批次持续使用早期批次产生的几何、部件库、验证器和已知限制。因此，在当前站点范围内，现有记录支持将 AstraBuild 描述为持续工程工作流；但这些证据不能说明该工作流在另一座变电站或其他重建领域中的迁移表现。

8.2 Validation changes representation choices8.2 验证会改变表示选择

Several revisions alter more than numerical parameters. B01/B02 changes the fitting and holdout domains, B15 narrows the reusable MASTER boundary, B23 changes a lead from a straight segment to a fitted curve, and D38 corrects the interpretation of review-markup orientation. Within these cases, validation and review act as inputs to subsequent representation decisions.

若干修订涉及的变化超过数值参数调整。B01/B02 修改拟合域与留出域，B15 缩小可复用 MASTER 的边界，B23 将引线从直线段改为拟合曲线，D38 则修正对审查标记朝向的解释。在这些案例中，验证和审查结果会直接进入后续表示决策。

8.3 Coverage and human review provide additional task signals8.3 覆盖度与人工审查提供额外任务信号

B25/B26 use unexplained photogrammetric occupancy after inventory-driven reconstruction to identify additional work. B36 and D38 use reviewer-provided omission or spatial signals to initiate new reconstruction steps. These mechanisms expand the sources from which the next task can be selected, but the present record does not quantify their benefit against alternative task-selection policies.

B25/B26 在台账驱动重建后利用未解释的摄影测量占据区域识别额外任务；B36 和 D38 则使用审查者提供的遗漏或空间信号触发新的重建步骤。这些机制扩展了下一任务的信号来源，但当前记录未对其相对于其他任务选择策略的收益进行受控量化。

8.4 Deterministic checks define the acceptance boundary8.4 确定性检查定义验收边界

The workflow does not use the reasoning model as the sole acceptance mechanism. Geometry residuals, history preservation, rigid-instance checks, contact or continuity checks, coverage audits, and review records provide independent execution-time constraints on the generated programs. Their scope remains limited by the available reference data: in particular, photogrammetric residuals measure agreement with a correlated reconstruction rather than absolute survey accuracy.

该工作流不以推理模型作为唯一验收机制。几何残差、历史保持、刚体实例检查、接触或连续性检查、覆盖审计和 review 记录共同对生成程序施加独立的执行期约束。这些检查仍受参考数据范围限制，尤其是摄影测量残差衡量的是与相关重建结果的一致性，而非绝对测绘精度。

9 · Limitations

Limitations and threats to validity局限性与有效性威胁

The current record supports a single-site longitudinal analysis and leaves several questions unresolved.

当前记录支持单站点纵向分析，同时保留若干尚未解决的问题。

Site scope. All reconstruction tasks come from one 220 kV substation. Task diversity within this site provides no direct estimate of cross-site generalization.

站点范围。 所有重建任务均来自同一座 220 kV 变电站，因此站内任务多样性不能直接估计跨站泛化能力。

Reference geometry. The photogrammetric mesh guides reconstruction and is also used in the reported reference-surface comparisons. The residual statistics therefore quantify internal agreement with that reconstruction, not independent survey accuracy.

参考几何。 摄影测量粗模既用于指导重建，也用于报告参考表面对照，因此残差统计衡量的是与该重建结果的内部一致性，而不是独立测绘精度。

Human intervention. Human decisions affect the B08 equivalence assumption, B36 omission review, D38 markup, and semantic audit. The present study describes a human-steerable workflow and does not estimate performance under fully autonomous operation.

人工介入。 B08 的同构判断、B36 的遗漏审查、D38 标记以及语义审计均受到人工决策影响。因此本文描述的是人工可引导工作流，并未估计完全自主运行条件下的性能。

Heterogeneous validation. Geometry, connectivity, civil reconstruction, correction, and semantic tasks use different validators. The historical record does not provide a common repeated-trial denominator from which a single success rate can be estimated.

异构验证。 几何、连接、土建、纠错和语义任务使用不同验证器。历史记录不存在可用于估计统一成功率的共同重复试验分母。

Model provenance and comparison. The Codex + GPT-6 Astra Extra High configuration is owner-confirmed because legacy batch manifests do not freeze model metadata. The current draft also contains no matched model baseline, human CAD baseline, independent LiDAR/survey reference, or second-site replication.

模型 provenance 与比较。 由于旧批次 manifest 未冻结模型元数据，Codex + GPT-6 Astra Extra High 配置目前由项目负责人确认。当前稿件也尚未包含 matched model baseline、人工 CAD baseline、独立 LiDAR/测绘参考或第二站点复现实验。
